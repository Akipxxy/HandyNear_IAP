## 2\. App Summary

HandyNear is a local handyman-booking web platform that connects customers with skilled handyman professionals for repair and maintenance jobs. Users can browse available handymen, view services by category, and submit a booking request; handymen can be discovered and contacted for jobs ranging from plumbing to electrical work. The app is built in React and currently includes Home, Browse, Contact, and How-It-Works pages.

## 3\. Part B Audit

**Things HandyNear stores (resources):**

* **Users** – customers who book services
* **Handymen / Providers** – workers offering services (name, skills, location, availability)
* **Services** – job types offered (plumbing, electrical, painting, etc.)
* **Bookings** – a request linking a user + handyman + service + time
* **Reviews** – ratings/feedback left after a job
* **Contact messages** – submissions from the Contact page
* **Categories** – groupings used to filter services/handymen

**Actions a user can take:**

* Browse / search handymen or services
* Filter by category, location, or availability
* View a handyman's profile/details
* Create a booking (request a service)
* Edit or cancel a booking
* Mark a booking complete
* Leave a review/rating
* Submit the contact form
* Sign up / log in (if auth exists)
