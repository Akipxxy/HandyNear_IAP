# 1. THE HARDEST PART OF THE LIFECYCLE
For me, i considered the hardest part of the lifecycle was the week 10 integraation part. During the presentation, a very crucial problem in the project as a whole. Initially, the team had decided to work with our upstream partner, MoneyMentor to help handymen in the HandyNear application to manage their expenses and guide them on good money habits such as saving but the problem came in when it displayed the handymens personal financial details. Luckily, one of the team members came up with another innovation of introducing a wallet that customers can cash money in when booking handymen services.At that moment it felt like all the work we had put in to the project was futile and also there were time constraints in implementing the idea. However, with the responsiveness of Team 6 to change things on their end and with us working together, we made the idea come to fruition.

# 3 What I would design differently
Knowing what I know now, I would take more time on the planning that would lead to integration with the upstream partner in the earlier stages of the project lifecycle. Initally, we had only brainstormed the idea for having a savings dashboard for the handymen, in almost less than hour. In retrospect, as we saw later on, that idea was not the best after all. I also feel that our week 6 endpoints would have been built more carefully so as to have a smoother testing phase.
